Terms of Service — rCLONE Sync

Last updated: October 2026

Purpose

rCLONE Sync is a personal configuration of the open-source rclone software. It is used by the account owner to synchronize selected files between a local computer and the owner's Google Drive account. This is not a publicly offered online service.

Authorization and file operations

Use of the integration requires the account owner to authorize Google Drive access through Google OAuth. Within the permissions granted and based on the synchronization settings selected by the owner, the tool may list, read, upload, update, rename, move, or delete files in Google Drive. The owner remains responsible for reviewing synchronization settings and ensuring they are authorized to synchronize the selected files.

Availability and responsibility

The integration is provided for personal use without any promise of uninterrupted availability. Backups and independent review of important files are recommended before changing synchronization settings. Google Drive, GitHub, and rclone have their own applicable terms.

Privacy

For information about the handling of Google Drive data and locally stored OAuth credentials, please read the Privacy Policy.

Affiliation

This personal integration is not an official Google product and is not affiliated with Google or the rclone project.
